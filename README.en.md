# Corporate Finance and Retirement Investment Strategy

**University of Sydney · FINC3600 Finance in Practice · Semester 2, 2023**

[中文首页](README.md)

This portfolio presents two academic team case studies: a proposed acquisition involving Treasury Wine Estates (TWE), and a retirement fund lifecycle strategy. It focuses on financial reasoning, assumptions and decision communication.

## 1. TWE acquisition and financing assessment

**Business question:** How would a proposed acquisition of Australian Vintage affect TWE's financing capacity?

The team considered strategic fit and a hypothetical financing proposal. My presentation sections covered leverage, liquidity, capital structure and WACC implications.

The presentation scenario showed interest coverage declining from **3.83× to 3.64×**, the quick ratio from **1.07× to 1.01×**, and the cash ratio from **0.52× to 0.45×**. These changes highlight the need to assess debt capacity alongside interest servicing and cash availability.

**Deliverable:** A financial impact assessment supporting a simulated board presentation.

[Read the case study in Chinese](cases/01-twe-acquisition.md)

## 2. Retirement lifecycle strategy

**Business question:** How should a default investment strategy account for members at different stages of working life?

My contribution covered model inputs, simulation runs and market-shock analysis, including starting balances and salaries for new and 50-year-old members, a 12% long-term contribution assumption, and a −37% market downturn scenario. The team compared lifecycle allocation with a uniform balanced strategy.

**Deliverable:** Member assumptions and scenario explanations supporting a simulated investment committee presentation.
